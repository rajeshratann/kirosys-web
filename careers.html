How to apply

Send your profile, portfolio or GitHub

Email support@kirosys.in with the track you are interested in, links to your work, and a short note about the kind of problems you enjoy solving. We read everything and reply to everyone we can take further.

Applying from outside Patna? Several of our tracks are open to remote and hybrid arrangements. Mention your preferred setup in your first email so we can be upfront about what is possible for that role.
